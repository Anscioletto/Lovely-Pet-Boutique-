# LovEly Pet Boutique: Shopify E-commerce for a Dog & Cat Grooming SPA

A Shopify store for **LovEly Pet Boutique**, a professional grooming salon and SPA for dogs and cats in Ventimiglia, Italy. It has 500+ active products across 22 collections, an in-house line of natural cosmetics, pages for the salon's services with online booking, and shipping to 32 European countries.

🔗 **Live store:** [www.lovelypetboutique.com](https://www.lovelypetboutique.com)

---

## 📌 Overview

LovEly Pet Boutique combines a **physical salon** (grooming, SPA, ozone therapy, hydromassage) with an **online shop**. The store has two jobs:

* **Sell online**: accessories, clothing, toys and the boutique's own natural products.
* **Promote the salon**: dedicated pages for dog and cat grooming and SPA treatments, plus a booking page.

## 💡 Key Takeaways

* **Online and offline together**: the store sells products and also brings customers to the salon, with service pages and a booking page.
* **A recognizable own brand**: each product in the natural cosmetics line has a short, memorable name, and bundles encourage customers to buy the whole routine.
* **Seasonal merchandising**: Christmas, Halloween and licensed collections (Disney, Marvel, Star Wars) keep the catalog fresh all year.
* **Search-friendly catalog**: descriptive product titles and keyword-rich tags.
* **European reach**: three active markets and shipping to 32 countries from a local salon.
